# Graph and subgraph mining

Frequent-subgraph mining searches labeled graph databases or a single large graph for recurring structures. The main difficulties are graph isomorphism, canonical representation, duplicate generation, and the far larger extension space compared with itemsets.

Apriori-style algorithms join smaller graphs; pattern-growth algorithms extend canonical embeddings. Variants mine trees, paths, closed or maximal subgraphs and can impose structural or application-specific constraints.

Source: `S6`, pp. 2–138.
