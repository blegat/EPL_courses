# Advanced clustering and biclustering

Fuzzy clustering assigns graded memberships rather than hard labels. High-dimensional clustering must cope with irrelevant dimensions and concentration of distances; subspace clustering and biclustering search for groups supported only by selected attributes.

Graph and network clustering uses connectivity rather than ordinary vector distance. Semi-supervised clustering incorporates must-link, cannot-link, or label information, while compressed and approximate patterns trade exactness for scale.

Source: `S10`, pp. 2–123.
