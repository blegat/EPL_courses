# Renewal rewards, Little's law, and M-G-1 queues

## Topics and results

- A renewal-reward process accumulates a reward determined within each IID
  cycle. The renewal-reward theorem gives long-run reward rate as expected
  reward per cycle divided by expected cycle length (`REWARD`, pp. 2, 8–9).
- Applying it to age, residual life, and containing-cycle duration yields the
  inspection-paradox formulas involving $E[X^2]/E[X]$ (`REWARD`, pp. 3–7).
- The limiting residual-life distribution and the key renewal theorem connect
  cycle rewards with ensemble averages (`REWARD`, pp. 10–13).
- Little's law states $L=\lambda W$: long-run average population equals
  throughput times average sojourn time, under the stated stability/finite-cycle
  conditions (`LITTLE`, pp. 1–10).
- For an M/G/1 queue, residual service and Poisson-arrivals-see-time-averages lead
  to the Pollaczek–Khinchine mean-waiting formula (`LITTLE`, pp. 11–13).
