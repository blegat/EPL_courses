# Poisson processes and exponential arrivals

## Topics and results

- A rate-$\lambda$ Poisson process is a renewal counting process with IID
  exponential interarrival times (`PP`, pp. 2–4).
- Exponential memorylessness makes the post-$t$ residual arrival time exponential
  and independent of the observed past (`PP`, pp. 5–9).
- Counts over disjoint intervals are independent and stationary; $N(t)$ has a
  Poisson distribution with mean $\lambda t$ (`PP`, pp. 9–13).
- Equivalent definitions use exponential interarrivals, stationary independent
  increments, or infinitesimal one-arrival probabilities (`PP`, pp. 11–14).
- Superposition adds independent Poisson rates, while independent thinning
  produces independent Poisson subprocesses with multiplied rates (`PP`,
  pp. 15–18).
- Conditional on $N(t)=n$, unordered arrival times are IID uniform on $(0,t]$
  and ordered arrival epochs are uniform order statistics (`PP`, pp. 27–31).
