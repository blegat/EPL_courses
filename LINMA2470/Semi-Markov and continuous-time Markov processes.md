# Semi-Markov and continuous-time Markov processes

## Topics and results

- A semi-Markov process combines an embedded Markov chain with transition-
  dependent random holding times (`MP`, pp. 2–3).
- Its time-stationary probabilities weight embedded-chain visit frequencies by
  mean holding times (`MP`, pp. 4–6).
- A continuous-time Markov process is the exponential-holding-time special case;
  transition rates form a generator $Q$ (`MP`, pp. 7–11).
- Chapman–Kolmogorov forward/backward differential equations govern transient
  probabilities; stationary probabilities solve $\pi Q=0$ (`MP`, pp. 10–15).
- Uniformization adds self-transitions to convert a CTMC transient calculation
  into a Poisson mixture of discrete-chain transitions (`MP`, pp. 16–17).
