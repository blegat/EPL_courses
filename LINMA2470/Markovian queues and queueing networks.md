# Markovian queues and queueing networks

## Topics and results

- M/M/1 is a birth–death CTMC with Poisson rate $\lambda$, exponential service
  rate $\mu$, utilization $\rho=\lambda/\mu$, and geometric stationary queue
  length when $\rho<1$ (`MP`, pp. 18–23).
- PASTA lets Poisson arrivals observe the time-stationary state distribution,
  supporting waiting-time calculations (`MP`, pp. 21–23).
- M/M/$s$ pools service capacity; Erlang-style stationary probabilities quantify
  delay and economies of scale (`MP`, pp. 24–27).
- Finite-capacity M/M/1/K introduces blocking, while finite-source M/M/1//N has
  state-dependent arrivals (`MP`, pp. 28–30).
- Detailed balance/time reversal yields Burke's theorem: in steady-state M/M/1,
  departures are Poisson and independent of the future queue state (`MP`,
  pp. 35–38).
- Serial, feedback, and open queueing networks inherit product-form structure
  under the stated Markovian assumptions (`MP`, pp. 39–42).
