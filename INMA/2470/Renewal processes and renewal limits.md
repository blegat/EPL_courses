# Renewal processes and renewal limits

## Topics and results

- A renewal process counts IID positive interarrival intervals; at a renewal
  epoch the future process restarts probabilistically (`RENEW`, p. 2).
- The strong-law renewal theorem gives $N(t)/t\to1/E[X]$ almost surely
  (`RENEW`, pp. 3–5).
- A renewal CLT gives asymptotically normal count fluctuations when the
  interarrival variance is finite (`RENEW`, p. 6).
- Time averages can be converted into averages over renewal cycles (`RENEW`,
  p. 7).
- Stopping times and Wald's equality relate a random sum to the expected number
  of IID increments under the stated conditions (`RENEW`, pp. 8–10).
- The elementary renewal theorem gives $E[N(t)]/t\to1/E[X]$; Blackwell's theorem
  gives the asymptotic renewal rate in a fixed interval for non-arithmetic laws
  (`RENEW`, pp. 11–15).
