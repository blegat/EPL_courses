# Non-Markovian queue approximations

## Topics and results

- General interarrival/service distributions usually destroy a finite Markov
  state description and closed-form queue solution (`NONMARKOV`, p. 2).
- The variability–utilization–time approximation combines squared coefficients
  of variation, utilization, and mean service time; it is exact for the stated
  M/M/1 and M/G/1 cases and approximate for G/G/1 (`NONMARKOV`, p. 2).
- A corresponding multiple-server approximation adds the effect of pooling and
  server count (`NONMARKOV`, p. 3).
- Non-exponential service or arrivals generally make departures non-Poisson;
  variability propagation approximations track the output coefficient of
  variation through networks (`NONMARKOV`, pp. 4–5).
