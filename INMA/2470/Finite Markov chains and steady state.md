# Finite Markov chains and steady state

## Topics and results

- A homogeneous discrete-time Markov chain is specified by a row-stochastic
  transition matrix; $n$-step probabilities are entries of $P^n$ (`FMC`,
  pp. 2–3, 8).
- Accessibility and communication partition states into classes; classes can be
  transient or closed, and periodicity constrains convergence (`FMC`, pp. 4–7).
- Stationary probabilities solve $\pi=\pi P$. For an ergodic finite chain,
  $P^n$ converges to rows equal to the unique stationary distribution (`FMC`,
  pp. 9–12, 16).
- Rewards attached to states or transitions give transient cumulative rewards,
  stationary average gain, and a relative gain/bias vector (`FMC`, pp. 13–18).
