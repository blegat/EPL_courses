## Relationship to the other courses

LEPL1109 supplies the probability, estimation, regression, and introductory
Gaussian-process background. LINMA1731 develops finite-sample estimator bounds,
classical and Bayesian optimal estimators, and recursive state estimation.
LINMA2470 studies stochastic processes from the queueing and decision-process
side, while LINMA2725 uses related state-space and stochastic-control models.
LELEC2885 applies Kalman and particle filters to visual tracking.
