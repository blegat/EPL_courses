# Nonuniform and circuit complexity

## Topics and results

- A machine with advice receives a length-dependent auxiliary string that need
  not be computable uniformly (`NOTES`, pp. 106–107).
- Boolean circuits are finite acyclic networks of gates; a circuit family has
  one circuit for every input length (`NOTES`, pp. 107–110).
- Circuit size and depth measure nonuniform resources. Polynomial-size circuit
  families define $P/poly$.
- Uniform polynomial-time computation yields polynomial-size circuits, while
  unrestricted advice can encode length-specific information unavailable to a
  uniform algorithm (`NOTES`, pp. 110–112).
- The distinction clarifies why a small circuit family is not automatically an
  executable algorithm for constructing those circuits.
