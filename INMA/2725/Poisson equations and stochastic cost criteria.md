# Poisson equations and stochastic cost criteria

## Topics and results

- The Poisson equation relates one-step cost, its invariant mean, and a relative
  value/potential function (`S67`, pp. 21–25).
- For ergodic finite systems, centering the cost and summing transition powers
  constructs a solution; solutions are defined only up to an additive constant
  (`S67`, pp. 22–25).
- Lyapunov drift inequalities provide stability and cost bounds beyond finite
  spaces (`S67`, pp. 26–28).
- Total, average, and discounted costs encode different horizons. Discounting
  makes the Bellman operator contractive, while average cost needs recurrence
  and normalization conditions (`S67`, pp. 27–31).
