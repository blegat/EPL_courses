# Linear quadratic Gaussian control

## Topics and results

- LQG augments linear dynamics with process and measurement noise and treats the
  state as only partially observed (`CH1`, p. 13).
- The conditional state estimate and covariance are propagated by Kalman-filter
  recursions (`CH1`, pp. 13–15).
- The separation principle combines the LQR feedback gain with the conditional
  mean estimate: estimation and control can be designed separately under the
  linear-Gaussian assumptions (`CH1`, pp. 14–15).
