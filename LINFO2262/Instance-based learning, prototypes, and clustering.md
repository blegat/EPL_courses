# Instance-based learning, prototypes, and clustering

- $k$-nearest neighbors predicts from labels of nearby stored cases; distance,
  scaling, and $k$ determine its bias–variance behavior (`INST`, pp. 3–12).
- Consistency results coexist with a curse of dimensionality: local neighborhoods
  require rapidly growing samples (`INST`, pp. 13–20).
- Search structures, approximate neighbors, and distance-weighted voting reduce
  prediction cost or refine influence (`INST`, pp. 21–27).
- Clustering groups unlabeled examples by similarity; K-means alternates nearest
  centroid assignment and centroid recomputation (`INST`, pp. 28–35).
- Prototype classification summarizes classes with learned representatives,
  trading storage and speed against approximation error (`INST`, pp. 36–42).
