# Regression specification and interpretation

The linear model writes the conditional mean as $E(y\mid X)=X\beta$. Specification determines the response, explanatory variables, transformations, intercept, and error assumptions. A coefficient is a partial association: it measures the change in the conditional mean for one predictor while the others are held fixed.

Functional form matters. Logs, polynomials, and interactions change both units and interpretation, and association becomes causal only under additional identification assumptions.

Source: `LM`, pp. 12–35.
