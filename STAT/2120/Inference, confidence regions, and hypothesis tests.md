# Inference, confidence regions, and hypothesis tests

Under Gaussian errors, standardized coefficient estimates follow Student distributions and the scaled residual variance follows a chi-squared distribution. This yields confidence intervals for coefficients and mean responses, prediction intervals for new observations, and joint ellipsoidal confidence regions.

Linear restrictions $R\beta=r$ are tested with $t$ or partial $F$ statistics. The extra-sum-of-squares principle compares nested models, linking coefficient tests, ANOVA, and general linear hypotheses.

Source: `LM`, pp. 123–179.
