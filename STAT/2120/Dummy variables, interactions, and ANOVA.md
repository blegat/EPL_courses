# Dummy variables, interactions, and ANOVA

Categorical predictors enter a linear model through indicator variables relative to a reference level. Including every category together with an intercept creates exact collinearity, so contrasts or a dropped reference category are required.

Interactions let a slope or group difference depend on another variable. ANOVA is a linear-model comparison: sums of squares and $F$ tests assess whether groups or interaction blocks improve the fit, with interpretation depending on balance and coding.

Source: `LM`, pp. 197–221.
