# Panel-data fixed and random effects

Panel data follow units over time. Pooled OLS ignores persistent unit heterogeneity; fixed-effects estimators remove time-invariant unit effects by demeaning or differencing, whereas random-effects models treat them as random and exploit both within- and between-unit information.

Random effects require the unit effect to be uncorrelated with regressors. Hausman-type comparisons assess this restriction. Serial correlation, heteroskedasticity, clustered uncertainty, and limited within-unit variation remain central practical concerns.

Source: `LM`, pp. 331–356.
