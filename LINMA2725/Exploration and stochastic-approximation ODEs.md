# Exploration and stochastic-approximation ODEs

## Topics and results

- Reinforcement-learning data must sufficiently explore relevant state–action
  regions; a feedback policy plus probing noise is used to generate training
  trajectories (`CH3B`, p. 16).
- Recursive parameter updates are cast as stochastic approximation with
  decreasing step sizes (`CH3B`, pp. 16–18).
- An associated ordinary differential equation describes the mean limiting
  dynamics. Stability of its equilibrium is used to analyze convergence of the
  discrete stochastic recursion (`CH3B`, pp. 18–19).
