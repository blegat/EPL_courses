# Markov systems, invariant measures, and ergodicity

## Topics and results

- A stochastic system is specified through a transition kernel; the kernel acts
  on probability measures and on functions via conditional expectation (`S67`,
  pp. 5–13).
- Stationary/invariant measures need not exist or be unique. Finite-state
  examples expose periodicity, reducibility, and transient behavior (`S67`,
  pp. 7–14).
- For a finite irreducible aperiodic chain, Perron–Frobenius spectral properties
  give a unique invariant distribution and convergence toward it (`S67`,
  pp. 15–20).
- Ergodicity justifies replacing steady-state expectations by long-run temporal
  averages, a foundation for trajectory-based learning (`S67`, pp. 15–20).
