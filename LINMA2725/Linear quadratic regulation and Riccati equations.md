# Linear quadratic regulation and Riccati equations

## Topics and results

- Discrete LQR controls $x_{k+1}=Fx_k+Gu_k$ under a positive-semidefinite
  quadratic state cost and positive-definite input cost (`CH1`, pp. 2–4).
- Backward dynamic programming preserves quadratic value functions and yields a
  linear state-feedback policy (`CH1`, pp. 5–7).
- The infinite-horizon solution is characterized by the discrete algebraic
  Riccati equation; under the stated assumptions its solution gives the optimal
  gain and a positive-semidefinite value matrix (`CH1`, pp. 8–9).
- Finite-horizon LQR follows a backward Riccati recursion with a terminal cost
  (`CH1`, p. 10).
- The continuous-time Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman calculation produces the
  continuous algebraic Riccati equation and linear optimal feedback (`CH1`,
  pp. 11–12).
