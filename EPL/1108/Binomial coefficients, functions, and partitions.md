# Binomial coefficients, functions, and partitions

## Topics and results

- Subsets of fixed size and binomial coefficients; Pascal's identity and the
  binomial theorem (`NOTES`, pp. 54–59; `COUNT`, pp. 21–27).
- Counting functions, injections and bijections; permutations and factorials
  (`NOTES`, pp. 60–63).
- Surjections by inclusion–exclusion and set partitions through Stirling
  numbers of the second kind (`NOTES`, pp. 64–67; `COUNT`, pp. 34–43).
- Derangements and $!n=n!\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k/k!$ (`COUNT`, pp. 44–47).
- Occupancy interpretations connect functions to distributing labelled
  objects among labelled boxes.
