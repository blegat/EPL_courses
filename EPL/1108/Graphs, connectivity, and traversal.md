# Graphs, connectivity, and traversal

## Topics and results

- Directed and undirected graphs, adjacency, incidence, degree, walks, trails,
  paths and cycles (`NOTES`, pp. 83–89).
- The handshake lemma gives $\sum_v\deg(v)=2|E|$ for an undirected graph.
- Adjacency lists and matrices offer different time/space tradeoffs; matrix
  powers count fixed-length walks (`NOTES`, pp. 90–92; `GRAPH`, pp. 39–46).
- Graph isomorphism preserves adjacency and graph invariants.
- Breadth-first and depth-first traversal discover connected components and
  produce search trees (`NOTES`, pp. 93–98; `GRAPH`, pp. 5–15).
- Eulerian circuits use every edge; Hamiltonian cycles visit every vertex
  (`NOTES`, pp. 103–107).
