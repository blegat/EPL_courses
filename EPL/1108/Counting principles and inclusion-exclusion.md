# Counting principles and inclusion-exclusion

## Topics and results

- Finite cardinality and bijective counting; sum and product principles for
  mutually exclusive and successive choices (`NOTES`, pp. 37–43).
- Inclusion–exclusion for two sets and its alternating-sum generalization to
  finitely many sets (`NOTES`, pp. 44–49; `COUNT`, pp. 11–16).
- Complementary counting and double counting as proof strategies.
- Uniform probability on a finite sample space satisfies
  $P(A)=|A|/|\Omega|$ (`COUNT`, pp. 5–10).
- Applications include words, selections with or without replacement and the
  birthday problem (`COUNT`, pp. 17–20, 48–51).
