# Bernoulli trials and discrete distributions

## Topics and results

- A Bernoulli variable records success with probability $p$; independent
  repetition defines a Bernoulli process (`PROBA`, pp. 35–40; `P1`, pp. 4–8).
- A count of successes is binomial:
  $$P(X=k)=\binom nkp^k(1-p)^{n-k},$$
  with mean $np$ and variance $np(1-p)$ (`PROBA`, pp. 41–44).
- The waiting time to the first success is geometric and memoryless
  (`PROBA`, pp. 45–47; `P2`, pp. 17–23).
- Infinite independent trials motivate almost-sure and limiting calculations
  (`P2`, pp. 10–16).
