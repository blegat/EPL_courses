# Exponential, Poisson, and normal laws

## Topics and results

- The exponential distribution is the continuous memoryless law:
  $P(X>s+t\mid X>s)=P(X>t)$ (`PROBA`, pp. 49–52; `P2`, pp. 24–31).
- $P(N=k)=e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$ defines a Poisson count with mean and
  variance $\lambda$ (`PROBA`, pp. 53–54).
- If $n\to\infty$, $p\to0$, and $np\to\lambda$, the binomial law converges
  to the Poisson law (`PROBA`, pp. 55–58).
- Poisson arrivals combine Poisson counts with independent exponential waiting
  times (`PROBA`, pp. 59–62).
- The normal family is affine-stable; standardization reduces calculations to
  the standard normal CDF (`PROBA`, pp. 63–66).
