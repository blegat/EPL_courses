# Generating functions and linear recurrences

## Topics and results

- A formal power series $A(x)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nx^n$ encodes a sequence; sums,
  products, shifts and coefficient extraction translate combinatorial
  constructions into algebra (`NOTES`, pp. 68–72; `GF`, pp. 3–10).
- Ordinary generating functions solve distribution and selection problems.
- Linear recurrences with constant coefficients become rational generating
  functions; partial fractions recover closed forms (`NOTES`, pp. 73–81;
  `GF`, pp. 11–18).
- Exponential generating functions use coefficients $a_n/n!$ and are natural
  for labelled structures (`NOTES`, pp. 76–78).
- Probability and moment-generating functions encode laws and turn sums of
  independent variables into products (`GF`, pp. 19–22).
