# Proofs, sets, relations, and induction

## Topics and results

- Propositions, implication, equivalence, negation and quantifiers; direct
  proof, contrapositive proof and proof by contradiction (`NOTES`, pp. 9–15).
- Set operations, Cartesian products, power sets and De Morgan's laws
  (`NOTES`, pp. 16–19).
- Binary relations; reflexivity, symmetry, antisymmetry and transitivity;
  equivalence classes and partial or total orders (`NOTES`, pp. 20–24).
- Functions, composition and inverses; injectivity, surjectivity, bijectivity
  and the pigeonhole principle (`NOTES`, pp. 25–29).
- Recursive definitions, well ordering and mathematical induction, including
  strong induction (`NOTES`, pp. 30–33).
