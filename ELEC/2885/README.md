## Relationship to the other courses

LEPL1109 supplies probability, least squares, Fourier intuition, and introductory
data analysis. LELEC2870 provides the machine-learning foundation—optimization,
SVMs, feature methods, and neural networks—used here for visual data. LINFO2262
and LINFO2364 overlap through classification, clustering, and evaluation, while
LDACS1110 supplies regularization and Monte Carlo ideas reused in sparse inverse
problems and particle tracking. LELEC2885 specializes these tools to images,
geometry, segmentation, recognition, and video tracking.
