# Support-vector machines and kernels

- Hard-margin SVM selects the separating hyperplane with maximal geometric
  margin; active training points are support vectors (`SVM`, pp. 3–11).
- The primal constrained quadratic program and its dual expose dependence on
  inner products (`SVM`, pp. 7–17).
- Kernels replace feature-space inner products and implicitly construct nonlinear
  boundaries; valid Gram matrices are positive semidefinite (`SVM`, pp. 18–24).
- Soft margins introduce slack and a penalty controlling margin violations
  versus regularization (`SVM`, pp. 25–29).
- Convex optimization yields a global optimum, while kernel/penalty selection
  remains a model-selection problem (`SVM`, pp. 30–33).
