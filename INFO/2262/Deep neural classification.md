# Deep neural classification

- A multilayer feedforward network composes affine transformations and nonlinear
  activations, extending the perceptron (`DEEP`, pp. 3–17).
- Softmax outputs and cross-entropy provide a probabilistic multiclass
  classifier (`DEEP`, pp. 14–20).
- Backpropagation computes layerwise gradients by the chain rule; stochastic
  gradient methods update parameters from minibatches (`DEEP`, pp. 21–31).
- Regularization, dropout, initialization, and normalization address overfitting
  and optimization difficulties (`DEEP`, pp. 32–39).
- Convolutional/recurrent architectures exploit spatial or sequential structure;
  deep models trade representation power for data, computation, and
  interpretability demands (`DEEP`, pp. 40–50).
