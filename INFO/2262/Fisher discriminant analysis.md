# Fisher discriminant analysis

- Fisher's criterion chooses a projection maximizing between-class separation
  relative to within-class dispersion (`GAUSS`, pp. 24–27).
- In the two-class case the optimal direction is proportional to the inverse
  within-class scatter times the mean difference (`GAUSS`, pp. 25–28).
- With equal Gaussian class covariances, Fisher's direction agrees with the LDA
  discriminant direction, although the derivations differ (`GAUSS`, pp. 28–30).
