## Relationship to other courses

LEPL1109 supplies introductory supervised learning, logistic regression,
resampling, and decision theory. LELEC2870 develops optimization, SVMs, neural
networks, and prototype methods in greater algorithmic detail. LINFO2262 focuses
on classification breadth and statistically sound evaluation. LINFO2364 reuses
classification in a broader data-mining setting.
