# Rich-data mining, privacy, and emerging trends

Modern mining covers text, graphs, streams, spatial and temporal data, and multimodal representations. Data augmentation and learned representations broaden model capacity, while causal questions require assumptions beyond predictive association.

Deployment introduces privacy, fairness, interpretability, robustness, energy, and objectives beyond average accuracy. Large language models change interfaces and representations but do not remove the need for provenance, evaluation, or responsible data use.

Source: `S12`, pp. 2–101.
