# Outlier and anomaly detection

An outlier is an observation inconsistent with an assumed normal pattern. Statistical approaches use parametric tails or non-parametric estimates; proximity approaches use neighbor distances or local density; learning approaches formulate one-class, reconstruction, or discriminative objectives.

Global and contextual anomalies differ, and high dimensions make neighborhoods unstable. Scores therefore require validation against the intended operational cost, contamination rate, and explanation needs.

Source: `S11`, pp. 2–122.
