# Sequential pattern mining

Sequential pattern mining replaces sets by ordered sequences of itemsets. Subsequence support remains anti-monotone, enabling candidate-based GSP, vertical-id-list SPADE, and projection-based PrefixSpan.

Practical variants constrain gaps, windows, length, or regular expressions. The representation must distinguish order within a sequence from simultaneous items within one event.

Source: `S5`, pp. 2–94.
